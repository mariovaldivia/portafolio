## Context

Internal information and processes were spread across different tools and departments. The company needed a single place to manage its day-to-day operations.

## What I did

- Led the development of the corporate intranet using **Symfony**.
- Brought the management of the company's key processes together on a single web platform:
  - people, vehicle and tool management,
  - workers' occupational documentation,
  - technical service: intake, assignment, diagnosis, technical report and sales report,
  - planning and tracking of field operations,
  - manufacturing, engineering and sales processes.

## Technical challenges

Bringing processes from different departments into one system, with a data model and workflows that served every team without losing what made each one specific:

- **Technical service** as a staged workflow (intake, assignment, diagnosis, technical report and sales report), where each step depends on the previous one and connects the technical and sales teams.
- **Occupational documentation** with expiry dates, so the company knows in advance which exams and licenses need renewal.
- **Operations planning** by client and site, with statuses that reflect the actual progress of each job.

## Outcome

- A single web platform for internal management that replaces scattered tools.
- End-to-end traceability of every technical service, from intake to sales report.
- Centralized control of workers' documentation and its expiry dates.
- Visibility into the planning and progress of field operations.
- A technology foundation of its own that the company can keep growing on.
