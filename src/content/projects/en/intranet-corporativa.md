## Context

Internal information and processes were spread across different tools and departments. The company needed a single place to manage its day-to-day operations.

## What I did

- Led the development of the corporate intranet on **Symfony**.
- Brought three key areas together on one web platform:
  - resource management,
  - risk prevention,
  - technical services.

## Technical challenges

Bringing processes from different departments into one system, with a data model and workflows that served every team without losing what made each one specific.

## Outcome

A single web platform for internal management that replaces scattered tools and gives the company its own technology foundation to keep growing on.
