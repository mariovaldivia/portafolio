## Context

Loading water into tanker trucks at filling points ("cachimbas") needed reliable tracking without relying on manual records.

## What I did

- Developed the monitoring platform with **Python/Django**.
- Integrated the software with **PLCs** to wirelessly capture data for every load.
- Automated the recording and visualization of each load.

## Technical challenges

Integrating software and hardware in the field: connecting the application to the filling-point controllers wirelessly and keeping a consistent record of every operation.

## Outcome

Automated monitoring of water loading into tanker trucks, replacing manual control with data captured directly from the hardware.
